One retirement away.

Eighteen counties have exactly one practicing dentist: 204,939 residents, 18 dentists, one for every 11,386 people. Nearly six times the statewide ratio. One retirement, relocation, or closure creates a new county with no dentist.

In Banks County, the only dentist is a specialist. There is no general dentist.
